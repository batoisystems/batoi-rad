# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and respectful community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome questions and differing technical opinions.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, and
other abusive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact described in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Project maintainers will review reports promptly,
protect confidentiality where practical, and may remove content or restrict
participation when necessary.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
